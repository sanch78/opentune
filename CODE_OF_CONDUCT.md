# Code of Conduct

OpenTune welcomes people of every background, identity, experience level, and
ability. Treat participants with respect and help make technical discussions
accessible to newcomers.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence with patience and courtesy.
- Give constructive feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect privacy and differences in experience or equipment.
- Keep discussion relevant and acknowledge the work of others.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are
not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
official project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close threads,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Responses should be
proportionate to the behavior and its impact; repeated or severe abuse may result
in immediate restriction. Maintainers must follow this policy as well.

## Reporting

Report concerns to repository owner [sanch78](https://github.com/sanch78) through
a contact method they make available on their profile. A dedicated private
project reporting channel has not yet been established. Do not post sensitive
details in public issues. If no private contact is available, use GitHub's
platform reporting tools for abusive content.

Reports should be handled discreetly, with identifying details shared only as
needed to investigate and act. This is a project-specific community policy.
